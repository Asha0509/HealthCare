## Recognise it
FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call emergency services. Other signs are sudden numbness on one side, sudden confusion, trouble seeing in one or both eyes, trouble walking, and a sudden severe headache.

## What to do
Call emergency services immediately and note the time symptoms started. Do not give food, drink or medicine. Treatment works best in the first few hours, so do not wait to see if symptoms pass.
