## Self-care
Tiredness often comes from poor sleep, stress, a recent illness or too little activity. Keep a regular sleep schedule, eat balanced meals, stay hydrated, limit caffeine and alcohol, and build up gentle exercise.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if tiredness lasts more than two weeks without a clear cause, or comes with weight loss, unusual thirst or urination, low mood, feeling cold all the time, or breathlessness. Blood tests can check for anaemia, thyroid problems or diabetes.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help if extreme tiredness comes with chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting, confusion, or thoughts of harming yourself.
