## Self-care
Most back pain is muscular and improves within a few weeks. Keep moving gently rather than staying in bed, use heat or cold packs, and an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed. Avoid heavy lifting until it settles.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if back pain does not improve after a few weeks, spreads down a leg below the knee, comes with numbness or tingling, or comes with fever or unexplained weight loss.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for back pain with loss of bladder or bowel control, numbness around the genitals or buttocks, sudden weakness in the legs, or back pain after a serious fall or accident.
