## Self-care
For a small cut, press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth for 10 minutes without lifting it, then clean and cover it. A nosebleed usually stops if you sit, lean forward and pinch the soft part of the nose for 10 to 15 minutes.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor for a wound that is deep, gaping or dirty, an animal bite, nosebleeds that keep coming back, or easy bruising and bleeding gums without a clear cause.

## Get emergency help if
Call emergency services for bleeding that will not stop with firm pressure, spurting blood, heavy bleeding, vomiting or coughing up blood, black tarry stools, or any bleeding during pregnancy.
