## Recognise it
Chest pain or pressure lasting more than a few minutes, pain spreading to the arms, back, neck or jaw, shortness of breath, cold sweat, nausea and light-headedness. Women, older adults and people with diabetes may have milder or unusual symptoms such as breathlessness or tiredness alone.

## What to do
Call emergency services right away. Sit down and rest while you wait. Do not drive yourself to hospital.
