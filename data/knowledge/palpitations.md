## Self-care
A brief pounding or fluttering heartbeat is often triggered by caffeine, stress, dehydration or lack of sleep. Sit down, breathe slowly, drink water and cut back on caffeine, alcohol and energy drinks.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if palpitations keep coming back, last more than a few minutes, or you have a known heart condition. A doctor may arrange an ECG.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for palpitations with chest pain, shortness of breath, fainting or near-fainting, or a very fast heartbeat that will not slow down.
