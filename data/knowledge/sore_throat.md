## Self-care
Most sore throats are viral and get better within a week. Drink warm fluids, gargle with warm salt water, suck lozenges (not for young children), rest your voice, and use paracetamol as directed if needed.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if a sore throat lasts more than a week, comes with high fever, white patches on the tonsils and swollen neck glands, or you have trouble swallowing liquids.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help if you have difficulty breathing, cannot swallow your own saliva and are drooling, or have a muffled voice with severe one-sided throat pain.
