## Recognise it
An overdose of medicine (taking too many pills or tablets, on purpose or by accident), swallowing a household chemical such as bleach, or breathing in fumes. Signs include drowsiness, confusion, vomiting, burns around the mouth, or difficulty breathing, but a person can look well at first.

## What to do
Call emergency services or a poison centre immediately, even if the person seems well. Keep the container or packet to show the medical team. Do not make the person vomit unless a professional tells you to.
