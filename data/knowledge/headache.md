## Self-care
Most headaches are tension-type headaches or migraines and settle with rest. Drink water, eat regular meals, rest in a quiet dark room, and use an over-the-counter pain reliever such as paracetamol exactly as the label says. Avoid using pain relievers on more than a few days a week, because that can cause rebound headaches.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor within a day or two if headaches are new and keep coming back, get steadily worse over days or weeks, wake you from sleep, come with vision changes, or follow a minor head injury. People over 50 with a new type of headache should also be checked.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for a sudden, explosive "worst headache of my life", a headache with fever and a stiff neck, confusion, weakness or numbness on one side, trouble speaking, a seizure, or a headache after a serious head injury.
