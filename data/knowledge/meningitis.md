## Recognise it
Fever with a severe headache, stiff neck, sensitivity to light, confusion or drowsiness, vomiting, and sometimes a rash that does not fade when a glass is pressed against it.

## What to do
Get emergency medical help immediately. Meningitis can get worse within hours and needs urgent treatment.
