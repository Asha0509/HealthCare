## Self-care
Brief dizziness when standing up quickly, from dehydration or after missing a meal, usually passes. Sit or lie down until it settles, drink fluids, and stand up slowly.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if dizziness keeps returning, comes with ringing in the ears or hearing loss, the room spins for long periods, or it started after a new medicine.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help if dizziness comes with a drooping face, slurred speech, weakness or numbness on one side, a sudden severe headache, chest pain, fainting, or double vision. These can be signs of a stroke or a heart problem.
