## Self-care
Mild stomach aches from indigestion, gas or a mild stomach bug often pass within a day or two. Sip water, eat small bland meals, avoid alcohol and fatty food, and rest. A warm compress can ease cramps.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if pain lasts more than a day or two, keeps coming back, comes with fever, burning when you pass urine, diarrhoea for more than two days, or unexplained weight loss.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for severe pain that comes on suddenly, pain with a rigid or very tender belly, pain in the lower right side that is getting worse, vomiting blood, black or bloody stools, or severe pain during pregnancy.
