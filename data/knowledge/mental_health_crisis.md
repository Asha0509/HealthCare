## What to do now
If you are thinking about ending your life or harming yourself, you deserve support right now. Call a crisis helpline such as iCall (9152987821) or Vandrevala Foundation (1860-2662-345, 24/7), or emergency services on 112. If you can, tell someone you trust and stay with them.

## Stay safe
Move away from anything you could use to hurt yourself, avoid alcohol and drugs, and keep talking to someone until the urge passes.
