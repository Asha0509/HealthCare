## Self-care
General body aches often come with viral infections like flu, after unusual exercise, or with poor sleep. Rest, drink fluids, take a warm bath and use an over-the-counter pain reliever as directed.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if body aches last more than a week, come with a high fever, a rash, joint swelling, or dark urine, or follow a tick or mosquito bite in an area with dengue or malaria.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help if body aches come with difficulty breathing, confusion, severe weakness, very little urine, or bleeding from the gums or nose with fever.
