## Self-care
Joint aches after activity or a minor strain usually improve with rest, a cold pack for the first day or two, gentle movement after that, and an over-the-counter pain reliever used as directed.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor for joint pain that lasts more than a couple of weeks, morning stiffness that lasts over an hour, swelling in several joints, or pain after an injury that stops you using the joint.

## Get emergency help if
Get urgent help for a joint that is suddenly hot, red, swollen and very painful with fever (possible infection), or a joint that looks deformed after an injury.
