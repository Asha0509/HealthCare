## Self-care
Mild breathlessness after exertion that settles quickly with rest is common. If you have a known condition such as asthma, follow your action plan and use your reliever inhaler as prescribed. Sit upright and breathe slowly.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor promptly for breathlessness that is new, getting worse over days, wakes you at night, comes with swollen legs, or comes with a cough that will not settle.

## Get emergency help if
Call emergency services if you cannot speak in full sentences, your lips or face turn blue or grey, breathlessness came on suddenly with chest pain, you are confused or very drowsy, or your inhaler is not helping during an asthma attack.
