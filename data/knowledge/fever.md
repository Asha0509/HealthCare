## Self-care
A fever is usually the body fighting an infection such as a cold or flu. Rest, drink plenty of fluids, wear light clothing and use paracetamol as directed on the label if you are uncomfortable. Check your temperature regularly and note how long the fever lasts.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if a fever lasts more than three days, keeps rising above about 39.4 C (103 F), comes with a painful cough, burning urine, ear pain, or a rash, or if you have a weak immune system. In areas where dengue or malaria are common, a fever with body aches should be tested.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for fever with a stiff neck, a rash that does not fade when pressed, confusion, severe headache, difficulty breathing, a seizure, or signs of severe dehydration. Any fever in a baby under three months needs urgent medical assessment.
