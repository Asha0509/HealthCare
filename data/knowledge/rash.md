## Self-care
Many rashes come from irritation or mild allergy. Stop any new soap, cream or detergent, keep the skin cool and dry, avoid scratching, and use a fragrance-free moisturiser or calamine lotion.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if a rash spreads, blisters, becomes painful or infected, lasts more than a week, or comes with fever or joint pain.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for a rash with swelling of the face, lips or throat, or difficulty breathing, or a rash that does not fade when pressed with a glass, especially with fever.
