## Self-care
Most coughs come from colds and settle within three weeks. Drink warm fluids, try honey in warm water (not for babies under one year), rest, and avoid smoke. Steam or a humidifier can help a dry cough.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if a cough lasts more than three weeks, you are coughing up yellow-green phlegm with fever, the cough is getting worse, or you have lost weight or have night sweats.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help if you are coughing up a lot of blood, have severe difficulty breathing, have chest pain, or your lips turn blue.
