## Self-care
Drink small amounts often. Oral rehydration solution is best after vomiting or diarrhoea. Avoid alcohol and very sugary drinks.

## Get help if
See a doctor promptly if you cannot keep fluids down, pass very little dark urine, feel dizzy when standing, or are very drowsy. Babies, young children and older adults become dehydrated faster.
