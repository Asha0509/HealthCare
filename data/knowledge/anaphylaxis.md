## Recognise it
Swelling of the face, lips, tongue or throat, wheezing or difficulty breathing, hives spreading over the body, dizziness or fainting, usually minutes after a food, medicine or insect sting.

## What to do
Call emergency services. If the person has an adrenaline auto-injector, use it as instructed. Lie them down with legs raised unless breathing is easier sitting up.
