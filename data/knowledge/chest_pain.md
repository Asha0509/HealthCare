## Self-care
Chest pain that is clearly linked to a pulled muscle, a cough, or heartburn after a heavy meal may settle with rest, but chest pain should not be self-treated until a heart cause has been ruled out. Sit upright, loosen tight clothing and avoid exertion while you decide what to do.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor the same day for chest pain that comes and goes with activity, pain that is new and unexplained, or chest discomfort in someone with diabetes, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, smoking or a family history of heart disease.

## Get emergency help if
Call emergency services for chest pain that is crushing, squeezing or heavy, pain spreading to the arm, jaw, neck or back, chest pain with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea or light-headedness, or chest pain lasting more than a few minutes. Do not drive yourself.
