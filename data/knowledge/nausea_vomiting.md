## Self-care
Vomiting from a stomach bug usually stops within a day or two. Take small, frequent sips of water or an oral rehydration solution, then move to bland food as you feel able. Rest and avoid strong smells.

## See a doctor soon if
See a doctor if vomiting lasts more than two days (one day for young children), you cannot keep fluids down for more than eight hours, you are passing very little urine, or you might be pregnant.

## Get emergency help if
Get emergency help for vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds, severe belly pain, a stiff neck with fever, confusion, or signs of severe dehydration such as fainting or no urine for many hours.
